# Quarterly Learning Performance Summary (Synthetic)

## Overall Statistics
- Average study time: 21 hours
- Team pass rate: 68%
- Most attempted certification: AZ-204
- Average practice score before passing: 78%

## Key Findings

### Study Hours vs Pass Rate
- Less than 15 hours studied → Pass rate: 35%
- 15-20 hours studied → Pass rate: 62%
- More than 20 hours studied → Pass rate: 84%

### Practice Score vs Exam Outcome
- Practice score below 65% → Very likely to fail
- Practice score 65-75% → 50% chance of passing
- Practice score above 75% → Very likely to pass

### Workload Impact
- Employees with more than 20 meeting hours/week → 40% lower completion rate
- Optimal learning: 12-18 meeting hours + 15 focus hours/week
- Morning learners complete 25% faster than evening learners

## Recommendations
1. Schedule learning during focus-heavy periods
2. Target 75% practice score before attempting exam
3. Break study into 1-2 hour daily sessions
4. Assign study buddies for employees with heavy workloads
